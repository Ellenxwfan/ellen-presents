About

Built for events that cannot go wrong.

A boutique corporate events firm in the San Francisco Bay Area, working with venture capital and private equity firms, family offices, and professional services companies. High-end events, managed end to end.

Most events don’t fail on the big decisions. They fail on the transfer that was fifteen minutes short, or the slide deck that arrived after the rehearsal ended.

Fifteen-plus years of investor meetings, annual general meetings, and executive retreats — across the Bay Area, Greater China, and Singapore.

The firm is deliberately small. You work with the person who plans your event, and the same person is there onsite when it runs.

Every engagement is a professional event — investor meetings, executive retreats, client receptions, and corporate hospitality — rather than a personal celebration.
